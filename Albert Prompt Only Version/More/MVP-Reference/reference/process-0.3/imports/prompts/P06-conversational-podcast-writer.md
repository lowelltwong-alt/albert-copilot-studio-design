# P06 · Write a rich, two-host case-preparation episode

Portable core 0.1.0 candidate; W08 composition adapter, not a silent change to Albert 3.3.1. Run [WF05](../flows/WF05-conversational-podcast.md). Inputs and output naming are in that flow. This is our reconstruction of a useful conversational format, not NotebookLM's private prompt.

## Lock before writing

Identify case, selected witness, audience, source/graph/witness-view revisions, intended duration, hosts and allowed disclosure. Default target for a rich source is 30–40 minutes including intentional practice pauses, with two complementary hosts. Record whether the source can sustain it. Preserve a short useful episode for thin evidence instead of inventing facts. Every factual sentence needs a source pointer in the nonspoken claim map. Source documents outrank graph summaries; verify important graph claims against Markdown. An edge, conflict row or strategy label is not proof.

## Research broadly; speak within the witness boundary

Read all authorized case Markdown, exhibits and statements for writer context. Build a source-indexed research ledger and assign every proposition a lane: `witness_observed`, `witness_later_learned` with timing, `attributed_disputed`, `author_only`, `unknown`, or `method`. Each also needs explicit `learner_allowed` yes/no and basis. Case rules may permit a statement to be heard without making it personal knowledge; do not assume that permission. Keep author-only strategy, rival testimony and hidden material in a separate author ledger, never the learner export, claim-map excerpt or recap. It can inform what to verify and which general pressure skills to teach; it cannot leak by question, denial, analogy or coaching hint. If a realistic question requires restricted content, mark it author-only and ask the owner to resolve the disclosure rule.

Build direct, cross, redirect and possible recross questions from permitted premises. Distinguish a supported likely line of questioning from a prediction or case fact. Do not invent counsel objectives, legal rules, documents, rulings or favorable explanations. A contradiction remains attributed accounts; it is not proof of deception. Use the supplied methods KB for technique; label method versus source. Practice truthful acknowledgment, uncertainty and correcting a premise. The hosts are not the witness, and do not put invented first-person testimony in their mouth.

## Plan a listening journey

Retain PC01–PC08: opening/role limits; role and timeline; evidence; direct; cross; conflicts/redirect; varied recall; close/navigation. Build a curiosity-led arc across those IDs; do not read these headings or a compliance checklist aloud. Select the handful of case tensions worth time and link all ten anchors KC01–KC10 to them. For each planned module list source propositions, host interaction, takeaway, retrieval opportunity, pause budget and estimated spoken words. Include adverse facts and real limits rather than only reassuring points. Missing knowledge or conflicting instructions stops the affected module for resolution.

## Write actual conversation

HOST_A and HOST_B are equal thinking partners. Both may explain, ask, challenge and admit a source limit. One introduces a concrete source-bound tension; the other follows its consequence, probes a tempting conclusion or tests it against the record. A follow-up must change understanding. Use connected exchanges rather than a stack of independent questions. Explain what the record supports before asking the listener to retrieve it later. Maintain accurate attribution naturally in speech.

Use plain spoken language, varied turn lengths, signposts that respond to what was just said, brief analogies that preserve limits, occasional genuine synthesis and a return to the opening tension. Interest comes from the evidence and distinctions. Avoid compulsory catchphrases, filler, theatrical insults, fake surprise, invented character psychology, two hosts saying the same thing, and repeated 'pause, say it, now check' narration. A longer turn is fine when its explanation earns the time. Do not imitate distinctive reference wording or copy its case facts/metaphors.

Include realistic direct/cross sequences with supported follow-ups, careful document handling, a source-bounded redirect where supported, and authentic listener response space. Give feedback from permitted facts after the pause; do not imply the unseen listener answered correctly. Vary practice: timeline reconstruction, document/observation distinction, false-premise detection, delayed recall and unhinted transfer. Keep production cues and citations outside spoken lines.

## Ten things this witness should know cold

Select exactly ten nonduplicative priority anchors from the permitted case material. Each has: ID, plain takeaway, source span, personal-knowledge/time limit, why it matters, associated likely question and chapter. A legitimate anchor can be 'what I cannot establish'; it must still be case-specific and evidenced. Teach each naturally earlier, then give a compact closing recall round and a separate one-page card. 'Know cold' means understand facts and limits, not memorize a polished answer or eliminate honest uncertainty. If ten cannot be supported, explicitly return fewer and `source_depth_shortfall`.

## Deliver and repair

Produce plan, complete PC01–PC08 dialogue, permitted claim map, KC card, separate author-only ledger, speech-only speaker export and timing sheet. Word-count spoken text; estimate `words / rate + intentional_pause_seconds / 60`, with a stated 130–160 words/min sensitivity range and 145 central assumption unless the brief specifies otherwise. These are planning assumptions, not a measured voice rate. Aim for useful depth; never fill the clock with repetition. A 30–40 minute estimate might require roughly 4,000–5,000 spoken words plus pauses, but actual totals govern.

Return issues and a factual self-check, not your own decisive quality grade. Separate A02 and P01 reviewers inspect the full frozen artifact. Accept only evidence-backed fixes, retain the original, update maps/exports after changes and recheck whole assembly once. No API, MCP, audio or external delivery is required. Operator saves/reopens exact files; model chat context alone is not storage.
