# Independent review and disagreement workflow

Use this workflow when an asset, prompt, harness, or outcome needs a second
judgment. The author records a concise candidate and evidence references. A
different reviewer tests the stated claim and records `needs_evidence`,
`actionable_candidate`, or `reject`. A disagreement remains visible to the
human owner; neither reviewer can promote a record.

For a claimed fix, a later recurrence is a verification signal. It is not a
frequency score, proof of severity, or permission to change the asset.
