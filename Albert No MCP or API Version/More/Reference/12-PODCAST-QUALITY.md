# Podcast priority and gold-reference comparison

The podcast is the primary user-facing quality target. The packet, chunks, graph and conflict ledger exist to make the script accurate, witness-specific and useful in spoken practice. Keep source claims, conflicting accounts, time/knowledge limits and exact evidence references through every transformation. Evaluate the finished script independently of the packet.

## Review sequence

1. Freeze the source/profile/evidence versions and the exact reviewed packet. Create a chapter plan before drafting.
2. Draft one chapter at a time with navigation, practical questioning, retrieval/recall pauses and truthful answer discipline. Keep citations, claim map and production notes outside spoken text. Do not script invented witness answers or inflate facts to meet length.
3. Freeze the complete script A0 and evaluate E0. Resolve targeted substantive findings for A1/E1 and again for A2/E2. Require two accepted material cycles; permit at most five revision attempts, then stop unresolved. Never count formatting or repeated scoring as an improvement.
4. Check the exact final packet/script pair against the source passages, including negations, uncertainty, evidence-only roles, exhibit retrieval and conflicts. A changed packet/source can require revising and rescoring affected podcast content.
5. Read the complete script aloud. Assess naturalness, information density, repetition, pause placement and the difference between a coach's explanation and facts a witness may state. Obtain human acceptance of the exact saved text.
6. If the owner supplies an authorized gold-standard script, register its version, applicable case/role, permitted use and rubric. Compare matched dimensions and whole-artifact usability; use blind labeling for human comparison where feasible. Without a supplied reference and actual review, report 'gold comparison not performed'. A high model score alone cannot establish equivalence.
7. If audio is later produced through an approved process, listen to the actual output for pronunciation, speaker labels, missing words, timing, pauses and factual changes. Text approval does not prove audio quality. Audio generation/submission has not occurred in this package.

## Eight podcast dimensions

- examination_readiness
- conflicts_impeachment_rehabilitation
- role_fidelity_truthful_answer_discipline
- timeline_exhibit_source_retrieval
- unseen_transfer_active_recall
- witness_specific_realism
- spoken_naturalness_read_aloud_readiness
- navigation_concision_non_repetition

The full-edition target is mean >= 4.25 AND every dimension >= 4.25, zero serious findings and all hard controls passing. Two material cycles apply separately to packet and script. The featured full script target is 8,000–12,000 spoken words (roughly 55–83 minutes before pauses at the illustrative planning rate of 145 words/minute). Do not expand a tiny synthetic excerpt with invented case material to reach that length. Return an explicit source/scope gap if a useful full script cannot be supported. The MVP intentionally produces a shorter demonstration and is not full-length or gold qualified.

Packet category mapping remains an owner decision for formal packet convergence. A complete human-reviewed diagnostic packet can inform a diagnostic script pilot, but must retain that status; do not bypass full release gates by relabeling it approved. Podcast evaluation uses its own eight dimensions. Gold qualification and demonstrated human learning require the separate authorized reference/panel/study evidence specified in the full implementation pack.

Save packet evaluations and podcast evaluations in separate logs. Record actual scores/findings, source passage IDs, resolved defects, regressions, reviewer/context identity, artifact versions and human decisions. In prompt-only editions these are operator-maintained records, not cryptographic or service-enforced receipts.
